Privacy Policy – Dial Deck

Effective date: 14 October 2026 · Last updated: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what the Android app Dial Deck (package name com.himavarshan.dialdeck, "the app") accesses on your device and how it is handled. The app is published by Himavarshan Reddy ("the developer", "I", "me"). You can contact me at himavarshanreddy@gmail.com.

Summary: Dial Deck shows your recent calls and your starred contacts on your home screen so you can call them with one tap. All of this happens on your phone. The app has no internet permission, does not collect your data, does not send anything to me or anyone else, and contains no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party software that collects data.

1. What the app does

Dial Deck is a home-screen widget. It displays a list of your recent calls and your favourite (starred) contacts. When you tap a name, the app asks your phone's calling service to start a call to that number. A "Phone" tab opens your default phone app.

2. Information the app accesses on your device

The app requests the following permissions. Each is used only for the purpose listed, and only when needed to draw or use the widget.

PermissionWhat it is used for
Call log (READ_CALL_LOG)To read your most recent calls (the number, the saved contact name if available, the time, and whether the call was incoming, outgoing or missed) and show them in the widget's Recents list so you can call back. The app never changes, adds or deletes call log entries.
Contacts (READ_CONTACTS)To read the contacts you have starred in your Contacts app (names and phone numbers) and show them in the widget's Favourites list.
Phone (CALL_PHONE)To place a call when you tap a name. The call itself is made by your phone's own calling service, not by the app.
Run at start-up (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)To restore the widget's refresh after your phone restarts. This does not give access to any personal data.

The app also asks Android to tell it when the call log changes, so the Recents list can refresh a few seconds after a call ends. This only tells the app that something changed. You can turn this refresh off in the app, and Recents will then refresh only when you use the widget.

3. How the information is used and where it stays

  • The information above is read and processed only on your device, and only to display the widget and place the call you ask for.
  • Names and numbers shown in the widget are displayed on your home screen by your device's launcher (home screen app), as with any widget.
  • The app does not request internet permission. It has no ability to send your information off your device and does not do so.
  • The app does not copy your call history or contact details into its own storage. The lists are read from Android each time they are needed.

4. What the app stores on your device

  • Your settings: theme, colours, how long names are shown, whether instant refresh is on, and which tab was last open.
  • The order in which you arranged your favourites, saved as internal contact ID numbers.

This is kept in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read under normal Android protections. It is removed when you uninstall the app. The app opts out of Android cloud backup, so this information is not backed up to your Google account by the app.

5. Data collection and sharing

  • The app does not collect personal information. Nothing is transmitted to me or to any server.
  • The app does not share, sell or rent information to any third party.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking, and no third-party software that collects data.
  • When you tap a name, the phone number is passed to your device's calling service to start the call you requested. This is an action you take on purpose and is not collected by the developer.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because I do not collect any of your data, there is nothing on my side to retain or delete. To remove everything the app stores on your phone, clear the app's storage in Android Settings > Apps > Dial Deck > Storage, or uninstall the app.

7. Your choices and controls

  • You can allow or revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings > Apps > Dial Deck > Permissions. Without the Call log permission the widget cannot show Recents. Without the Contacts permission it cannot show Favourites. Without the Phone permission it cannot place a call directly.
  • You can remove the widget from your home screen or uninstall the app at any time.

8. Security

Your information is not sent anywhere, which removes the risks of transmitting or storing it on a server. It remains protected by your device's own security, such as your screen lock and Android's app isolation. Please keep your device and Android version up to date.

9. Children's privacy

Dial Deck is intended for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Your rights

Laws in some places (for example the GDPR in the EU and UK, and the CCPA/CPRA in California) give you rights to access, correct or delete personal data held by a company. Because the app does not collect or hold your personal data, there is no data for me to provide, change or erase. If you have a question or concern, you can contact me at the address below.
